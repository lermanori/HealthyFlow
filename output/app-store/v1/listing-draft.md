## Description

Your day is full of things that belong together: work to finish, habits to keep, meals to log, and time to move. HealthyFlow brings them into one place.

Talk or type naturally to capture a plan, log what happened, or ask about your day. Review suggested changes before saving them. Open Today to see what's planned, what's done, and what comes next.

Features:
• One day, one timeline — bring tasks, habits, calendar events, meals and workouts together.
• Talk it through — use text or voice to capture ideas and turn them into actions you can review.
• Start with a photo — turn a meal photo or written plan into an editable suggestion.
• Make room for your day — see available time based on your schedule, with missing details made clear.
• Keep health in context — log food, calories, weight and workouts alongside the rest of your day.
• Connect your calendar — include events from your iPhone calendars with your permission.
• Check Today at a glance — see a compact view on your Home Screen widget.

Start without an account. Plan and record your day offline; AI and account services need an internet connection.

HealthyFlow is free, with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Start with 10 AI actions. Create and verify an account for 15 actions each calendar month. Text requests use 1 action, photos 5, and premium models 10. When your allowance runs out, you can keep planning and logging manually.

Your day is stored on this device. Cloud backup and transfer are not available to new users in this release. Deleting the app or losing your device can mean losing that data. AI submissions and analytics are handled as described in our privacy policy.

Your whole day. One place.
