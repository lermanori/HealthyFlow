How it works

One day. One timeline. It carries itself forward.

No rigid forms to fill. You describe the day; HealthyFlow keeps it.

Step 01

Say what's on today

Tasks, meals, training, weigh-ins — typed or spoken, in plain language. No forms, no categories to pick.

Step 02

It lands on the day

Tasks get times, habits recur, meals get macros, weight goes on the curve. Not five apps. One day.

Step 03

Tomorrow picks it up

Whatever you didn't finish rolls forward on its own. No re-typing yesterday, no guilt list.

The product

One day, holding everything you keep in five apps.

Real screens from HealthyFlow — this is the actual product, not a concept.

Talk

Speak it. It's planned.

Type or dictate what's ahead of you and it becomes structured, scheduled items — right category, time, and duration. Then keep talking: Talk answers questions about your own day.

  • Natural language in, structured plan out
  • Voice input for hands-free capture
  • Ask about today, this week, or how a habit is going
Health

One Health tab, three ways to look after yourself

Nutrition, Workouts, and Progress live together instead of three apps in your sidebar. Log a meal in plain language and it breaks into calories and macros; weight charts itself; each one lands back on today's timeline.

  • Meal AI parses food into calories, protein, carbs and fat
  • Weight log with trend and latest-vs-previous delta
  • Track any measurement you care about, with or without a target
Workouts

Log training in seconds

Sets, reps, weight, time, distance — logged fast with quick-insert from your exercise history. Save a session you repeat as a plan and start from it next time. Your training lives next to your plan, not in another silo.

  • Quick insert from recent and most-used exercises
  • Reusable plans — strength, calisthenics, running, mobility
  • Session history by day, and on the timeline
