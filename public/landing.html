How it works

One day. One timeline.
It carries itself forward.

No rigid forms to fill. You describe the day; HealthyFlow keeps it.

STEP 01

Say what's on today

Tasks, meals, training, weigh-ins — typed or spoken, in plain language. No forms, no categories to pick. Whatever's in your head goes in as-is.

STEP 02

It lands on the day

Everything takes its place on one timeline — tasks get times, habits recur, meals get macros, weight goes on the curve. Not five apps. One day.

STEP 03

Tomorrow picks it up

Whatever you didn't finish rolls forward on its own. No re-typing yesterday, no guilt list. The day ends; the plan survives.

Today's Timeline

The timeline is the day's record

Everything that belongs to a day earns a place on the clock. Tasks and habits sit at their planned time; the meal you logged, the weigh-in, the workout, each bit of habit progress get stamped at the hour they actually happened. What's left in the Anytime backlog is only what still needs a decision from you.

  • Plans and records on one clock, in real order
  • Anytime backlog holds just the undecided — drag it onto an hour
  • Incomplete items roll forward on their own
Talk

Speak it. It's planned.

Type or dictate what's ahead of you, and the analyzer turns it into structured, scheduled items — with the right category, time, and duration. Then keep talking: Talk answers questions about your own day.

  • Natural language in, structured plan out
  • Voice input for hands-free capture
  • Ask about today, this week, or how a habit is going
Health

One Health tab, three ways to look after yourself

Nutrition, Workouts, and Progress live together under a single Health workspace instead of three separate apps in your sidebar. Log a meal in plain language and Meal AI breaks it into calories and macros; weight charts itself; each of them lands back on today's timeline.

  • Nutrition — Meal AI parses food into calories, protein, carbs & fat
  • Weight log with trend chart and latest-vs-previous delta
  • Progress — track any measurement you care about, with or without a target
Workouts

Log training in seconds

Sets, reps, weight, time, distance — logged fast with quick-insert from your exercise history. Save a session you repeat as a plan and start from it next time. Your training lives next to your plan, not in another silo.

  • Quick insert from recent and most-used exercises
  • Reusable plans — strength, calisthenics, running, mobility
  • Session history by day, and on the timeline

HealthyFlow started as the system I built for myself. I had tasks in one app, habits in another, calories in a third, workouts in a fourth — and a calendar that knew nothing about any of them. Every day began with app-switching instead of doing.
