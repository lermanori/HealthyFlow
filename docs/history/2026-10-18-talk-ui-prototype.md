# Talk UI prototype decision — 2026-10-18

Five mobile Talk directions were compared against the App Store ad and the product target. Direction D was selected: one ongoing assistant relationship, presented as a feed organized by day, with text, voice, and attachments sharing one composer.

The production Talk screen now keeps the newest conversation active for AI context and shows older saved conversations as read-only earlier days. Existing history is preserved without exposing separate chat selection. The AI's conversational behavior and durable understanding of preferences remain a separate follow-up task.
